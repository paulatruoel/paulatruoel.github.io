About the seminar ⤴

The seminar focuses on preprints (not your own!) that have recently appeared in the 'Geometric Topology' and related sections on the arXiv. 'Recently' can be interpreted as within the last one to two years, but we won't be strict. Here are some details:

Time: Wednesday, 2-3pm
Place: Seminar room 311B
Format: A 30-45 minute talk with plenty of time for questions.
Instructions for speakers: Make sure your audience can understand the title of the preprint you are presenting. Tell us about the main result or key concepts of the paper and/or why you chose it.
Reasons to give a talk: It's a great way to ensure you read that paper you're passionate about, and it's a good opportunity to get in touch with others and tell them what you're up to.
